# מפת המפלגות 2026 · Party Map 2026

Interactive, non-partisan review of the thirteen leading lists in the election for the 26th Knesset (27 October 2026): positions on five topics, a security/economy compass, credibility and professionalism scores, corruption and public-money indices, and short party histories. Hebrew, English and Russian.

Single static page (`index.html`) plus image assets. No build step, no server, no tracking.

## Images

All logos and photographs come from Wikimedia Commons. Logos are used for identification only and belong to the parties. Photographs are under Creative Commons licences; the author and licence of each file are listed at the bottom of the page and in `assets/manifest.json`.

## Disclaimer

Scores and axis positions are editorial estimates based on the sources cited on the page. Not an official document of any party.
